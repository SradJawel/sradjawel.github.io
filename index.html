HiveWatch

Designer and builder

A hive-monitoring system that reports weight, temperature and humidity so a beekeeper can check readings from a phone without opening the hive.

I built it around an ESP8266, environmental sensors and load cells, with readings sent to Supabase.

ESP8266, AHT20, HX711, Supabase
